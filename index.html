Who we are

Leadership starts young. Here, it starts with us.

Salima Youth Leadership Initiative (SYLI) is a youth-led organisation in Salima, the lakeshore district of Lake Malawi. We are students, health workers, farmers, graduates and volunteers who got tired of waiting for permission to make our district better.

We train young leaders, walk with them through mentorship, and support the ideas that turn into real projects — from a first workshop to a chilli harvest that pays school fees.

Change doesn't begin with wealth, power or position. It begins with people who dare to make a difference.

What drives us

A clear mission, a stubborn vision

Not a slogan on a wall — the test we hold ourselves to every term.

01

Our mission

To equip young people in Salima with the skills, confidence and support they need to lead change in their own communities — and to make sure their voices are heard where decisions are made.

02

Our vision

A Salima where every young person is trusted to lead, where ideas turn into action, and where the district's future is shaped by the people who will live in it.

03

Our values

  • Youth-led, not youth-adjacent
  • Community-rooted
  • Integrity in small things
  • Service over status
  • Togetherness — no one rises alone
